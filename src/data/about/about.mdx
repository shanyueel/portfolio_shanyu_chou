## Personal Journey

<Timeline>

<TimelineItem title="Building a Foundation in User-Centric Design" duration="2023 - Present" location="Taipei, Taiwan">

My career began as a CMF Designer, where I honed a meticulous eye for detail and a deep understanding of users. I conducted market trend analysis and collaborated in cross-functional teams to prototype and present new laptop designs, which strengthened my ability to communicate complex ideas to diverse stakeholders. This foundation in design thinking and detail-oriented execution now informs my work in creating intuitive, pixel-perfect user interfaces.

</TimelineItem>

<TimelineItem title="From crafting physical products to digital experiences" duration="2022 - 2023" location="Taipei, Taiwan">

Inspired by the agility and direct impact of software development, I transitioned my focus from physical products to digital experiences. I attended intensive online bootcamp to master frontend fundamentals. This journey culminated in the creation of my first side-project 'Wild Sync', a full-stack outdoor activity platform I built independently from scratch using React, Redux, and Firebase, solidifying my passion for solving users' problems with code.

</TimelineItem>

<TimelineItem title="Driving E-commerce Innovation as a Front-End Engineer" duration="2023 - Present" location="Taipei, Taiwan">

As a Front-End Engineer at Wabow, I develop and maintain features for a large-scale e-commerce platform. My work involves close collaboration with designers and backend engineers to transform product requirements into responsive and robust user interfaces. To date, I have successfully shipped over 10 major features, including a new 'rental service' module and a 'data warehouse' interface.

I am also spearheading the modernization of our internal management system, refactoring legacy jQuery code into a modern Vue 3 and TypeScript stack. This involves analyzing existing functionalities, defining clear API specifications with the backend team, and skillfully negotiating technical trade-offs to balance scope, complexity, and project timelines.

This role has sharpened my ability to diagnose and resolve production issues efficiently, enhancing my skills in managing different development workflows and prioritizing tasks effectively.

</TimelineItem>

<TimelineItem title="A Commitment to Lifelong Learning and Growth" duration="Future" location="Worldwide">

My core motivation remains the same: creating products that deliver an exceptional user experience. To build a deeper technical foundation, I dedicate my personal time to studying core computer science principles like data structures, algorithms, and discrete mathematics through Oregon State University. My goal is to apply this knowledge to write more efficient and scalable code. I am excited by the future of the web and am eager to contribute my unique blend of design and engineering skills in a collaborative, international environment.
