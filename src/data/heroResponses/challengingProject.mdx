<HeroCardContainer>
  The most challenging project I've led was **integrating AI into WACA's frontend development workflow**. The original process was slow and rigid: feature development waited on designer
  prototypes, and a legacy jQuery and Laravel codebase kept us tightly coupled to the backend team. Everything was linear, and shipping anything new took far longer than it should have.

  <HighlightBox iconName="LuTarget" title="The Real Challenge" variant="negative" className="mt-4">
    My goal was to cut idea-to-market time **without interrupting projects already in progress**. I quickly realized the hard part wasn't the technology. The hard part was getting the entire team
    with different backgrounds and skill levels to **agree on a new way of working**.
  </HighlightBox>

  <HighlightBox
    iconName="LuWrench"
    title="The Technical Approach"
    variant="neutral"
    className="mt-4"
  >
    - **Shared AI conventions**: markdown instruction files and skill configurations, so every team member's output followed the same structure and the AI stayed useful across the whole workflow.
    - **A documented component library**: the most concrete deliverable. Storybook-based, with AI-readable documentation so both developers and models knew exactly how to use the components we already had.
    - **MPA over SPA**: a Single Page Application would have meant a full rewrite and disrupted ongoing work, so my backend colleague and I migrated section by section instead, keeping the live service running throughout.
  </HighlightBox>

  <HighlightBox
    iconName="LuHandshake"
    title="The Hardest Part: Alignment"
    variant="info"
    className="mt-4"
  >
    I didn't get much resistance from the backend team since the new workflow made their old full-stack work much easier. But the designers were skeptical. When I first proposed that they **reusing existing components** to generate prototypes with AI, they were concerned about losing their creative freedom. But once I showed them how much time they could save by combining reusable components with AI prototyping, they came around. That shift alone transformed how fast we moved from concept to implementation.
  </HighlightBox>

  <HighlightBox iconName="LuRocket" title="The Result" variant="positive" className="mt-4">
    Delivery time dropped by **at least 70%**. More importantly, the time we saved let us introduce **AI-generated interactive prototypes** into the process. It was something we could never afford
    before. By reusing existing components to build prototypes for new features, we could validate usability early and make sure the features we shipped were more thoughtful and valuable to users.
  </HighlightBox>
